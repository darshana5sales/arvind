Introducing Highgrove

Close enough for a breeze, far enough to get away

Highgrove by Arvind — premium weekend homes where you can live the high life. Nestled amidst sylvan green surroundings, Highgrove has everything it takes to make weekends truly exciting and memorable for the whole family. Clean, green, open surroundings will take your breath away; feast your eyes on migratory birds during the season.

There is a lavish clubhouse with all facilities for rest and recreation — a large swimming pool, indoor and outdoor games and restaurants serving sumptuous cuisine. Developed with foresight and great attention to detail, Highgrove offers a weekend home that gives you a great escape today, and the promise of appreciation tomorrow thanks to its strategic location.

FAQs

Arvind Highgrove — your questions answered

Where is Arvind Highgrove located?

Arvind Highgrove is at Moti Devti – Moraiya Road, Moti Devti Village, Ahmedabad. On the Moti Devti – Moraiya Road, near Sanand and the Changodar industrial belt, with easy access to SG Highway and the city's west.

What type of project is Arvind Highgrove?

Arvind Highgrove is weekend golf villas and plots around Ahmedabad's largest shallow water-lily pond.

What amenities does Arvind Highgrove offer?

Amenities include Executive golf, Swimming pool, Net cricket, Tennis court, Squash court, Gymnasium, Spa, Steam & sauna, 40,000+ sq ft clubhouse and Restaurant. See the full list of 16 amenities on this page.

How far is Arvind Highgrove from key places?

Approximate distances / travel times: Sanand — 5 km; Gulmohar Greens — 5.5 km; Changodar Industrial Estate — 7 km; Shantipura Circle — 14 km; Decathlon — 15 km. Actual times vary with traffic.

What villa sizes are available at Arvind Highgrove?

Five villa types (total carpet area incl. balcony/verandah): Studio 62.46 sq yd, 1 BHK 100.51 sq yd, 2 BHK 166.66 sq yd, 3 BHK 205.39 sq yd and 4 BHK 256.98 sq yd. RERA carpet areas are 49.56, 86.51, 136.21, 181.28 and 218.64 sq yd respectively.

What is special about Arvind Highgrove?

Ahmedabad's largest shallow water-lily pond, spread over 3 acres with 50+ varieties of lilies, which attracts 200+ species of migratory birds, plus executive golf and a 40,000+ sq ft clubhouse.
