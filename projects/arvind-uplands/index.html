The Project

Live the weekend life, every day

Today's lifestyle involves living in cramped urban spaces five days of the week, broken only by occasional trips to weekend homes for some much-needed rest and recreation. Imagine if you could live the weekend life every day amidst green, natural surroundings — your children growing up playing outdoors while you reconnect with nature.

Uplands is an invitation to build a lifestyle that would be the envy of your peers: luxurious villas set in clean, green, open spaces; architecture that takes full advantage of the surroundings; best-in-class amenities for fitness and recreation; concierge services that make life easier — all close to the city's upcoming commercial hubs.

Green Living
Lifestyle of Health & Sustainability

A little green oasis on the city's edge

Nestled a little away from the periphery of the city, Uplands has been designed as a little green oasis — expansive surroundings, landscaped gardens, wildflowers and water bodies all around, rich with local flora and fauna. For the first time in Gujarat, Uplands introduces LOHAS — a concept of giving back to nature what you take from it.

  • Landscaped gardens & wildflowers
  • Water bodies all around
  • Butterfly garden & lush thicket
  • Walkways & promenades

Executive Golf
Live on the Links

Two minutes from your first tee

The Executive Golf Course is designed to let you indulge in the greatest game whenever the fancy takes you. Nestled right in the centre of Uplands, it is just a two-minute walk from your bungalow — its elegant design will challenge your golfing skills again and again. Take it all in from one of the strategically placed benches and watch the world go by.

  • 9-hole executive golf course

  • Benches to sit back & watch the world go by

The Golf Square
Premium Golf & Lifestyle Club

Something for everyone

Overlooking the lush green of the golf course is a premium club. For the sporty there's table tennis, squash, billiards, badminton, lawn tennis, basketball and volleyball; for movie buffs, a multimedia theatre for up to 30 people; for the quieter kind, an expansive library and an international-class spa. There are 13 five-star hotel suites, a banquet hall for 500 guests, a poolside party lawn for another 500 — and an electrifying discotheque.

The Villas
Designed by Woods Bagot

Five villa designs, each around water

Uplands offers a choice of 5 different villas, designed by the world-renowned architectural firm Woods Bagot, Hong Kong. Each villa is built around a water body and offers different configurations to suit your requirements — you can even add a personal swimming pool. Expect imported marble and wood finishes, crafted with loving attention to detail.

Fun Square — a Disney-themed world for children*

FAQs

Arvind Uplands — your questions answered

Where is Arvind Uplands located?

Arvind Uplands is at Shilaj – Rancharda Road, Nasmed near Rancharda, Ahmedabad. On the Shilaj–Rancharda Road at Nasmed — a little away from the city's periphery, yet close to its upcoming commercial hubs, so you're never far from work or play.

What type of project is Arvind Uplands?

Arvind Uplands is woods Bagot-designed villas around a 9-hole executive golf course.

What amenities does Arvind Uplands offer?

Amenities include 9-hole executive golf course, The Golf Square club, 13 five-star hotel suites, Swimming pool, Table tennis, Squash, Billiards, Badminton court, Lawn tennis and Basketball. See the full list of 28 amenities on this page.

How far is Arvind Uplands from key places?

Approximate distances / travel times: Sanand–Kadi Road — 10 min; Delhi Public School — 20 min; Shilaj Circle — 25 min; Taj Skyline — 25 min; Palladium Mall — 30 min. Actual times vary with traffic.

Who designed the villas at Arvind Uplands?

The villas are designed by the global architecture firm Woods Bagot, Hong Kong. There are 5 villa designs, each built around a water body, with an option to add a personal swimming pool.

Does Arvind Uplands have a golf course?

Yes. A 9-hole executive golf course sits at the centre of Uplands, a two-minute walk from the villas. The golf course and golf club are independent commercial entities, with membership on the operator's terms.
