FAQs

Arvind Sensora — your questions answered

Where is Arvind Sensora located?

Arvind Sensora is on Metal Road, near the Ahmedabad–Rajkot Highway, approximately 20 km before Nalsarovar. The exact site is pinned on the map on this page.

What plot sizes are available at Arvind Sensora?

Plots range from 400 to 800+ sq yards. Pre-launch pricing is shared for 400, 550, 675 and 800 sq yard plots.

What is the pre-launch price of Arvind Sensora plots?

Pre-launch prices: 400 sq yd ₹33.05 L, 550 sq yd ₹44.67 L, 675 sq yd ₹54.35 L and 800 sq yd ₹64.05 L. Special prices with club waiver: ₹31.30 L, ₹42.92 L, ₹52.60 L and ₹62.30 L respectively. Launch prices will be ₹36.00 L, ₹49.50 L, ₹60.75 L and ₹72.00 L. Pre-launch pricing is limited to the first 300 EOIs.

What is included in the price, and what is extra?

Prices include the basic land rate, development charges, club charges and maintenance, as stated in the pre-launch price sheet. PLC (Preferential Location Charges) and applicable government charges are extra. Special club waiver prices are subject to the applicable offer terms.

How do I book — what is the EOI amount?

Expressions of Interest (EOI) are being accepted now with an EOI amount of ₹50,000. Register on this page, call +91 97121 31519 or WhatsApp us to know plot availability and the EOI process.

When will Arvind Sensora launch?

The launch is expected in 1–2 months (as of October 2026). Pre-launch pricing is available only until the first 300 EOIs are received.

What amenities will Arvind Sensora have?

A premium clubhouse with a garden, 2 natural ponds and gated community-style amenities across a sprawling 50+ acre land parcel.
