Green Living

The weekend life, every day

Today's lifestyle means cramped urban spaces five days a week, broken only by the occasional trip to a weekend home. Arvind Uplands turns that around — a little green oasis a short way from the city where you live the weekend life every day, amidst landscaped gardens, wildflowers and water bodies.

Luxurious villas set in clean, green, open spaces, with best-in-class amenities for fitness and recreation and even concierge services to make a comfortable lifestyle easier. For the first time in Gujarat, Uplands introduces LOHAS — Lifestyle of Health and Sustainable Development — giving back to nature what you take from it, to create a living habitat that thrives in positive spaces.

01

Home is where the heart is

A choice of five villa designs by the world-renowned architectural firm Woods Bagot, Hong Kong. Each villa is built around a water body, with the option to add a personal swimming pool — finished in imported marble and wood crafted with loving attention to detail.

02

The Golf Square, a club for everyone

Overlooking the lush green of the course is a premium golf and lifestyle club. Table Tennis, Squash, Billiards, Badminton and Lawn Tennis for the sporty; Basketball and Volleyball for those who like to sweat it out; and a multimedia theatre seating up to 30 for special screenings.

03

Multiple ways to reinvigorate

A fully equipped gymnasium, swimming pool and an international-class spa to wear off the stress. There are 13 five-star hotel suites, a banquet hall that hosts 500 guests with ease, an outdoor poolside party lawn for another 500, and an electrifying discotheque for your peppy parties.

04

Awaken your senses

For the quieter kind, an expansive library to lose yourself in. The Zen Square is a secluded, elevated haven for meditation — open to nature, suffused with natural sights and sounds, surrounded by art installations that carry meaning and thought, and arrest all your senses.

05

For the food lovers

A cool café and a chic restaurant within the club, and — adjoining the Zen Square — the Zen Café, serving up a carefully crafted selection of Italian cuisine to please the gourmet in you.

Your own Disneyland

Fun Square at Uplands is a Disney-themed world spread over 54,000 sq.ft. — a playground and kids' club with Disney-inspired indoor and outdoor games and rides. An amphitheatre, a library, a souvenir shop and a café sit alongside slides, a tunnel, a fountain and a mascot park. The Disney vibe greets your children at the gate — the perfect place to allow them a memorable childhood.

More to explore: Executive Golf Course · Zen Square & Zen Café · Fun Square (Disney Kids Park) · Butterfly Garden · Landscaped Lakes & Water Bodies · Walkways & Promenades · Premium Concierge Services.
