Three islands, one city

Welcome to a 500+ acre integrated township that blends serenity, luxury and exclusivity seamlessly — a city within a city, designed for those who seek an elevated living experience.

Crafted with care and inspired by water, Arvind Aqua City redefines what modern living can be. From an expansive central lake and three enchanting islands to exquisite water villas and a world-class golf course, it offers not just homes but a lifestyle never before experienced in Ahmedabad.

The Central Lake

The serene heart of the estate

At the core of this extraordinary estate is a grand central lake, offering a serene setting for an unmatched living experience. With its tranquil waters and lush surroundings, it anchors three distinct islands — each with its own unique purpose.

Isle of Leisure

A grand clubhouse at the heart

At the heart of this island stands a grand clubhouse — a true embodiment of luxury and community. From a state-of-the-art fitness centre to gourmet dining, it is the perfect space for recreation, relaxation and celebration.

Boundless Leisure

Endless reflections

Experience a haven of curated clubhouse activities designed for your enjoyment. Revel in top-tier leisure at the clubhouse, soothe your soul at the spa, find tranquillity through yoga, or engage in meaningful conversations at the outdoor café.

Isle of Holistay

A resort by the water

Surrounded by the pristine waters of the lake, the resort offers beautifully designed rooms for those seeking an escape into peace and luxury. Perfect for weekend getaways or hosting visitors, it provides an intimate connection to nature with a rich suite of amenities.

Isle of Nirvana

A meditation island

Embark on a unique spiritual experience through a one-of-its-kind meditation centre in a tranquil, natural setting. Surrounded by water on all sides, it provides a space of serenity, reflection and calm — reconnect with yourself as the soothing sound of water reinvigorates your soul.

Water Villas

Life on the water

Step into a world of unparalleled elegance that epitomises luxurious living. Nestled on the lake, these grand villas offer a rare fusion of modern design and tropical allure — private balconies, exquisite finishes and uninterrupted views of the tranquil water.

Tradition, sport & serenity

Kashi Ghat. Complementing this expansive development are 11 beautifully designed temples — including a Jain Derasar — nestled on our kashi ghat. These sanctuaries of peace and reflection add a touch of tradition and spirituality to the modern living environment.

The Golf Course. Spanning a lush 38 acres, the 18-hole golf course offers a world-class experience for seasoned golfers and enthusiasts alike — an oasis of sport, luxury and natural beauty, set against the serene backdrop of the lake.

Sustainable by design, connected by location

Sustainability. The central lake is a natural reservoir vital to an eco-friendly design — capturing rainwater for irrigation and conserving local water resources, while naturally filtering pollutants and creating spaces for relaxation.

Location. Ideally located at Kalyangadh and well connected via NH-47 (the Ahmedabad–Rajkot highway), Arvind Aqua City offers a global, waterfront lifestyle — resort living, but everyday.
