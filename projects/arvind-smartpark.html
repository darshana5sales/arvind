Presenting Arvind SmartPark

Positioned for scale, speed and success

Arvind SmartPark is a 440-acre Grade-A industrial landmark strategically located on NH-47, the Ahmedabad–Rajkot Highway. Planned with plug-and-play infrastructure, the park is designed to enable faster project execution, seamless operations and scalable growth for modern industries.

With direct highway frontage, multimodal connectivity and integration with key freight corridors, the park offers an optimal balance of logistics efficiency, regulatory readiness and long-term operational resilience. Modular plot sizes are crafted for all industries — from emerging SMEs to established enterprises.

CETP · ZLD
Built for High-Compliance Industries

Zero liquid discharge, zero compromise

A 1.5 MLD Common Effluent Treatment Plant with Zero Liquid Discharge, expandable up to 6 MLD, makes Arvind SmartPark a preferred destination for high-compliance industries. Centralised wastewater treatment eliminates the hassle for individual units, ensures effortless environmental compliance and supports 24×7 operations.

  • Ideal for pharma, textiles & food processing
  • 100% wastewater treated & reused in the park
  • Plug-and-play — no individual ETP set-up
  • Professional O&M with a shared cost model

Connectivity
Prime Highway & Freight Corridor Advantage

On NH-47, minutes from the freight corridor

NH-47 is a seamless freight corridor linking Rajkot's textile and engineering clusters to Ahmedabad's markets and export hubs. The Ahmedabad–Dholera Expressway (NH-751) interchange at Sindhrej is 22 km away, the Western Dedicated Freight Corridor is reached via an expressway link, and Sanand Railway Station is 40 km away.

  • Immediate NH-47 frontage
  • 12 km to the expressway interchange for the WDFC (Delhi–Mumbai)
  • 22 km to the Ahmedabad–Dholera Expressway
  • Mundra & Kandla ports for export-driven units
