Built for industry at scale

Arvind SmartPark is a 440-acre Grade-A industrial landmark strategically located on NH-47, the Ahmedabad–Rajkot Highway. Planned with plug-and-play infrastructure, the park is designed to enable faster project execution, seamless operations and scalable growth for modern industries.

With direct highway frontage, multimodal connectivity and integration with key freight corridors, it offers an optimal balance of logistics efficiency, regulatory readiness and long-term operational resilience.

Modular plot sizes are crafted for all industries — from emerging to established, from SMEs to large enterprises. Phase one, consisting of approximately 207 acres, is being launched.

Amenities that accelerate industrial ease

An admin building with meeting and conferencing facilities, planned canteen, worker dormitories and on-site convenience stores, an integrated weighbridge, dedicated truck bays, a medical centre and on-site fire safety.

Crafted for zero wastewater

A 1.5 MLD Common Effluent Treatment Plant with Zero Liquid Discharge, expandable to 6 MLD. 100% of wastewater is treated and reused within the park — ideal for pharma, textiles and food processing, and ready for future-proof, ESG-aligned growth.

Positioned for movement

Set at Kalyangadh on NH-47, Arvind SmartPark sits at the heart of Gujarat's freight and logistics network — direct highway frontage, freight-corridor access and a dense ecosystem of ports, GIDCs and expressways.

Expressway interchange (Western Freight Corridor) — 12 km · Ahmedabad–Dholera Expressway interchange — 22 km · SP Ring Road — 35 km · SG Highway — 40 km · Sanand GIDC — 40 km · Sanand Junction on the WDFC — 60 km · Sanand Railway Station — 40 km · Ahmedabad city centre — 50 km · Ahmedabad International Airport — 60 km · Kandla Port — 315 km · Mundra Port — 350 km.

Distances and travel times are indicative and approximate, based on publicly available information.

Surrounded by giants

A thriving manufacturing neighbourhood with a deep vendor ecosystem of thousands of MSMEs within a 50 km radius — established engineering, plastics and agro-processing clusters at Bavla & Bagodara, the Kerala GIDC, and Sanand GIDC just 25 minutes away.

Industrial neighbours include Tata Motors (Sanand), Ford, Hitachi, Zydus and Intas Pharmaceuticals (Matoda), Corona Remedies (Bavla), Atul Auto (Bhayla), Inox Group (Rohika), and multinationals like Nestlé, P&G, Coca-Cola and Micron in Sanand.
