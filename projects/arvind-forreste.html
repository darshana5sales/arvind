The forest living

In nature, forests are an ecosystem designed to nourish every life form — fertile, fecund places abuzz with activity, where life thrives in all its glory.

Forreste by Arvind, in its true spirit, is a lush green living space that not only fosters coexistence with nature, but an adventure one embarks upon — a life different from the mundane. An exclusive premium villa scheme that actually endeavours to be a tropical forest.

01

A worthwhile adventure

What city life takes away, Forreste restores. Set foot here and disconnect from the noise — your children chase butterflies and hide in tree houses while you watch life go by from benches under the thicket, or dip tired feet in lily ponds.

02

Club Forreste, the heart of it all

The clubhouse at Forreste is a complete experience in itself. Family activities or solo relaxation, this centrepiece of life at Forreste has been designed to suit your every need — uninterrupted entertainment, the lifestyle of course.

03

Leisure, redefined

Laze around the pool and let your worries slip away, or walk the trail as the sights and sounds of nature calm you. Indoors, fill the need for something active with Table Tennis, Badminton and Billiards — or indulge in the Spa cum Salon.

04

The Perch — dine with the birds

The Forreste club boasts a one-of-a-kind outdoor café & restaurant — The Perch. With the birds to keep you company, soak in the flavours of the outdoor while you indulge the gourmet in you. A living space for every mood and every family member.

05

A perfect retreat

The villas at Forreste are built to suit your modern lifestyle and secure your green spot. Airy and well lit on the inside, surrounded by vast forest-like tree-lined trails on the outside — the perfect weekend retreat for capital appreciation that takes care of you in the long run.

A forest of experiences

Beyond the villas lies a curated forest of moments — a Tree House to climb, Forest Trails to wander, hidden Forest Caves to discover, and a Campfire Point for evenings under the stars.

Wander into the Butterfly Park, gather at the Amphitheatre, rest at the Forreste Sit-outs, or set up at the Adventure Camp beside the Forreste Lake. Every corner is an invitation to slow down and reconnect.
