The Forest Living

A life different from the mundane

In nature, forests are an ecosystem designed to provide nourishment and sustenance to all life forms — fertile, fecund places where life thrives in all its glory. Forreste by Arvind, in its true spirit, is a lush green living space that not only fosters coexistence with nature but is also an adventure one embarks upon.

When your busy schedule makes you long for a pause, Forreste is your grand escape to a world long forgotten. Drawing inspiration from the world's greatest artist — Mother Nature — it creates sights and smells, sounds and experiences that draw you in and revitalise you. A weekend stolen away for a quick recharge of the right kind benefits you today, and leads to capital appreciation that takes care of you in the long run.

FAQs

Arvind Forreste — your questions answered

Where is Arvind Forreste located?

Arvind Forreste is at Jethlaj, Rancharda–Khatraj Road, Ahmedabad. Forreste sits close to commercial and industrial hubs, schools, hospitals and important city landmarks — and just minutes from Thol Bird Sanctuary, which is the icing on the cake.

What type of project is Arvind Forreste?

Arvind Forreste is an exclusive premium villa scheme that endeavours to be a tropical forest.

What amenities does Arvind Forreste offer?

Amenities include Reception, Café, Restaurant, Lounge with seating & library, Kids zone, Banquet hall, Board games, Badminton court, Gym and Spa & salon. See the full list of 34 amenities on this page.

How far is Arvind Forreste from key places?

Approximate distances / travel times: Alpha College of Engineering & Technology — 2 min · 0.5 km; Arvind Ltd. Campus — 2 min · 1 km; Mother Teresa World School — 10 min · 4 km; Thol Lake — 11 min · 5.5 km; Arvind Uplands — 15 min · 11 km. Actual times vary with traffic.

What forest experiences are there at Arvind Forreste?

A forest tree house, forest trail, forest caves, campfire point, forest sit-outs, a butterfly park and an adventure camp. These are offered as additional amenities for allottees and are not part of the project.

Is Arvind Forreste RERA registered?

Yes. GujRERA: RAA06788 · RAA10718 (gujrera.gujarat.gov.in).
