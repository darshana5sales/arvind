FAQs

Arvind Aqua City — your questions answered

Where is Arvind Aqua City located?

Arvind Aqua City is at Kalyangadh, Ahmedabad. Ideally located at Kalyangadh on the Ahmedabad–Rajkot Highway (NH-47), Arvind Aqua City offers a global, waterfront lifestyle with a host of sports and recreational facilities and a luxury clubhouse — a fuller life, close to the city.

What type of project is Arvind Aqua City?

Arvind Aqua City is a 500+ acre lakeside township — water villas, plots and an 18-hole golf course. Offering: Water villas & plots.

What amenities does Arvind Aqua City offer?

Amenities include A grand central lake, Swimming pool, Kids' pool, Lily pond, Luxury clubhouse & 5 sectoral clubhouses, Restaurant, Cafeteria, Multi-purpose hall, Party lawn and Theatre room. See the full list of 43 amenities on this page.

How far is Arvind Aqua City from key places?

Approximate distances / travel times: from the 6-lane Ahmedabad–Rajkot Highway (NH-47) — 700 m; drive from SG Highway, Ahmedabad — 30 min; from Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel International Airport — 45 min; Sarkhej · Changodar · Bavla · Kalyangadh — Via NH-47. Actual times vary with traffic.

What are the three islands at Arvind Aqua City?

Isle of Leisure, with a grand clubhouse; Isle of Holistay, a lakeside resort with rooms for residents and their guests; and Isle of Nirvana, a one-of-its-kind meditation centre surrounded by water on all sides.

Does Arvind Aqua City have a golf course?

Yes. An 18-hole golf course spread over 38 acres meanders through the estate, set against the lake and surrounding greenery.
