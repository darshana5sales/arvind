An address of legacy

The search for one's home often begins with a dream — a dream for a space that transcends time, where our stories unfold, where our children grow, and where our hearts find peace.

Step into Arvind Everland, a thoughtfully planned 151-acre lush green estate where pristine nature surrounds you and every moment feels serenely timeless. This is not just a community, it's an eternal haven that only a fortunate few will call home — the forever you've been waiting for.

A life in nature

Endless green views

Wake up to the calm of nature, where flamingos glide and the air feels lighter. Just a short drive from Ahmedabad, discover a life surrounded by over 200 species of birds and endless green views, moments away from the Nalsarovar Bird Sanctuary — peaceful walks and mindful living, crafted for comfort, calm and quiet sophistication.

Where arrival becomes an experience

A grand, gated entrance

As you approach the grand entrance, you're greeted by a stunning interplay of architecture and nature. Framed by landscaped greens, the wide avenue leads to an elegantly designed gateway — a bold statement of sophistication and serenity, and your gateway to peace, privacy and refined living.

Where life unfolds naturally

A canvas for your dream home

These plots aren't just pieces of land — they're canvas spaces for your aspirations: a weekend home, a forever family nest, or a peaceful retreat. Thoughtfully laid out across lush green surroundings, whether you choose to build now or later, your space remains your sanctuary, forever.

A world of indulgences

A grand clubhouse, the soul of the community

At the heart of Arvind Everland is a grand clubhouse where elegance meets luxury. Unwind in resort-style guest rooms, find calm in a serene yoga session, or share a gourmet evening with friends. Every detail speaks of luxury; every space invites you to slow down, reconnect and live well.

Endless moments of merriment

Resort-style pool & deck

Take a refreshing dip in the resort-style pool, lounge on the timber deck overlooking endless greens, or gather on the party lawns — Arvind Everland is designed for every generation to relax, play and unwind. Endless moments of merriment, yours forever.

A clubhouse that redefines indulgence

Indoors. A reception and circulation lounge, library, restaurant, indoor gym, party / banquet hall and resort-style guest rooms — alongside a pool table, table tennis and board games for unhurried evenings with family and friends.

Outdoors. A swimming pool with deck, kids' pool, pickleball and multipurpose courts, jogging track, star-gazing deck, rock climbing, a tree house, jungle trail, flower garden, lily pond and party lawn — curated experiences for every age, set amid pristine nature.

A closer look

Connected to the city, living in exclusivity

Escape to Arvind Everland, a tranquil sanctuary just over an hour from Ahmedabad, Gujarat's dynamic cultural capital — where refined seclusion meets effortless connectivity along the Sanand–Nalsarovar Road, near Mankol Cross Roads.

With pristine destinations like the Nalsarovar Bird Sanctuary close by, Everland offers the perfect blend of luxury, nature and stillness — an exquisite escape for those who seek peace without compromise. A project by Arvind SmartSpaces, part of the 125-year-old Lalbhai Group.
