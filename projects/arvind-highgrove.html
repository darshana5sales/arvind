Live the high life

Highgrove by Arvind — premium weekend homes nestled amidst sylvan green surroundings, close enough to make travelling a breeze and far enough to get away from it all.

Highgrove has everything it takes to make weekends truly exciting and memorable for the whole family. Clean, green, open surroundings, an executive golf course, a lavish clubhouse and a host of indoor and outdoor games — everything at hand so you can focus on recharging your batteries.

A weekend home here doesn't just give you a great escape, but also great appreciation of property over time. Say hello to great weekends filled with happiness — enhancing your lifestyle while also enhancing your networth.

01

Exit blues. Enter greens.

Leave behind the stress of the weekdays. Come Saturday, reward yourself with a scintillating game of golf — Highgrove's executive golf will inspire and challenge the golfer in you, while your family cheers you on.

02

Indulge to the point of feeling guilty

A sprawling clubhouse with a built-up area of more than 40,000 sq ft has everything to keep you busy for the weekend — indoor & outdoor games like net cricket, squash and gymnasium, a large swimming pool, restaurants and many more.

03

A feast for the eyes

The piece-de-resistance — Ahmedabad's biggest shallow water-lily pond, spread over 3 acres with more than 50 varieties of lilies. Each season it welcomes more than 200 species of migratory birds from far-off lands.

04

Performing under pressure is passé

Never let anything come between you and your game. Net cricket to polish your swings, lawn tennis to wield the racquet like a warrior's sword, and squash to upskill your craziest shots — get your kick within at the gymnasium too.

05

Weekends will never be the same again

Think beyond luxury, beyond the tangible. A spectacular sprawl of villas — designed by global architects Woods Bagot — give generous views of the project's focal point: the shallow water-lily pond, clubhouse and executive golf.
